## 🏆 The 12 ELITE Criteria

This isn't just technical analysis. This is **institutional-grade logic** that detects when smart money is positioning for a major move.

### 1️⃣ **Weekly Timeframe Bullish**
- Price above weekly EMA 20 & 50, OR
- Weekly RSI < 40 (oversold reversal setup)
- **Why it matters:** Weekly trend = the BIG picture. If weekly isn't aligned, forget it.

### 2️⃣ **Daily Timeframe Bullish**
- Price above daily EMA 20, 50, AND 200
- All EMAs in perfect order (20 > 50 > 200)
- **Why it matters:** Stock must be in clean uptrend on all major timeframes.

### 3️⃣ **4H Bullish Structure**
- Forming higher lows (consolidation before breakout)
- Breaking above recent 4H high
- **Why it matters:** Confirms the structure is building for a move.

### 4️⃣ **1H Timeframe Bullish**
- Price above 1H EMA 20
- **Why it matters:** Entry timing - ensures you're not buying into a pullback.

### 5️⃣ **Relative Strength vs SPY**
- **THIS IS THE SECRET WEAPON**
- Stock must be outperforming SPY by 5%+ over last 20 bars
- **Why it matters:** Why buy a stock doing +5% when the market is +10%? You want stocks LEADING the market, not following it. This filters out 90% of mediocre setups.

### 6️⃣ **Volume Surge (Institutional Activity)**
- Volume 50%+ above 50-day average
- **Why it matters:** Retail traders move thousands of shares. Institutions move MILLIONS. When volume explodes, smart money is entering.

### 7️⃣ **Money Flow Positive**
- Chaikin Money Flow > 0.1 (strong buying pressure)
- On Balance Volume trending up (accumulation)
- **Why it matters:** Price can lie. Volume can lie. But money flow tells you WHO is buying - and if it's institutions, you want in.

### 8️⃣ **Base Breakout**
- Stock consolidated in tight range (< 10% range) for 15-40 bars
- NOW breaking out above resistance
- **Why it matters:** ALL big moves come from consolidation bases. Find the base, find the breakout, find the profit.

### 9️⃣ **MACD Bullish & Rising**
- MACD above signal line
- BOTH lines rising (momentum building)
- **Why it matters:** Confirms momentum is accelerating, not fading.

### 🔟 **ADX Trending**
- ADX > 25 (trend in place)
- DI+ > DI- (uptrend confirmed)
- **Why it matters:** Don't trade choppy ranges. Only trade when a strong trend exists.

### 1️⃣1️⃣ **RSI Favorable**
- RSI < 30 (oversold entry), OR
- RSI 30-50 (early recovery from oversold)
- **Why it matters:** Best entries are when stock is hated (oversold) but structure is bullish.

### 1️⃣2️⃣ **Bullish Price Action**
- Bullish engulfing candle, OR
- Inside bar breakout, OR
- Gap up with volume
- **Why it matters:** Final confirmation - price action shows bulls taking control.
