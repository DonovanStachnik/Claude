# 📋 Quick Reference Guide - Swing Trading Indicator

## ⚡ Signal Checklist (4+ Required)

| # | Criterion | Buy Signal | Sell Signal |
|---|-----------|------------|-------------|
| 1 | **1D Trend** | Uptrend or reversal base | Downtrend or reversal top |
| 2 | **4H Structure** | Higher lows forming | Lower highs forming |
| 3 | **1H VWAP** | Price reclaims VWAP | Price loses VWAP |
| 4 | **Volume** | 20%+ above average | 20%+ above average |
| 5 | **Suite Diamonds** | 2+ blue diamonds | 2+ pink diamonds |
| 6 | **Green Mountain** | Strength building | Strength exhausting |
| 7 | **RSI** | <30 or recovering | >70 overbought |
| 8 | **MACD** | Bullish cross | Bearish cross |
| 9 | **Stochastic** | Bounce in range | Top in range |
| 10 | **ADX** | >25 strong trend | >25 strong trend |

## 🎯 Entry Rules

**When BUY signal appears:**
1. ✅ Verify score ≥ 4/10 on label
2. ✅ Check status table (top-right) for green checkmarks
3. ✅ Calculate position: **5-8% of account**
4. ✅ Set **HARD STOP at -10%** (shown on chart)
5. ✅ Set alerts for targets

**When SELL signal appears:**
1. ✅ Exit position immediately
2. ✅ OR if signal against your long position, tighten stops

## 📊 Exit Strategy

| Event | Action | % to Exit |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **Stop Loss Hit (-10%)** | EXIT ALL | 100% |
| **Target 1 (+10-15%)** | TRIM | 30-40% |
| **After Target 1** | MOVE STOP | To breakeven |
| **Trail Remainder** | ATR or Support | Hold |
| **Sell Signal Appears** | EXIT ALL | 100% |

## 🚨 Never Trade If:

- ❌ Score < 4 criteria
- ❌ Earnings within 3 days
- ❌ Major news expected
- ❌ Already in 3+ positions (overextended)
- ❌ Can't set a clear stop
- ❌ Position would be >10% of account

## 🎨 Chart Reading

**Visual Elements:**
- 🔵 **Teal Triangle + "BUY HERE"** = Entry signal
- 🔴 **Pink Triangle + "SELL HERE"** = Exit signal
- 🟡 **Yellow Line** = VWAP (dynamic support/resistance)
- 🔴 **Red Line** = Stop loss (-10%)
- 🟢 **Green Lines** = Targets (+10%, +15%)
- 📊 **Top-right table** = Real-time indicator status

## 🧠 Key Concepts

**Suite Diamonds:**
- Blue below zero = Best (early reversal buy)
- Blue above zero = Late trend buy
- Pink above zero = Best (early reversal sell)
- Pink below zero = Late trend sell

**Green Mountain:**
- Building = Momentum accelerating (good for buys)
- Fading = Exhaustion coming (exit warning)

**VWAP:**
- Above VWAP = Institutional support (bullish)
- Below VWAP = Institutional resistance (bearish)
- Reclaim = Strong buy signal

## ⏱️ Timeframe Hierarchy

1. **1D** = Trend direction (don't fight it)
2. **4H** = Structure (higher lows/lower highs)
3. **1H** = Entry timing (precision)

**Best setups:** All 3 aligned in same direction

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**No signals?** → Lower min criteria to 3

**Too many false signals?** → Raise min criteria to 5-6, increase ADX to 30+

**Getting stopped out often?** → Increase stop to 12-15% for volatile stocks

**Missing good moves?** → Review missed signals, lower volume % requirement

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Best signals:** Score 6-8/10 (don't wait for 10/10)
2. **Patience pays:** Wait for your setup, don't force trades
3. **Journal everything:** Track wins, losses, and lessons
4. **Size down early:** Start with 3-5% until confident
5. **Trust the stop:** Don't move it wider, ever
6. **Take profits:** Don't be greedy, trim at targets
7. **Market first:** Technical analysis doesn't beat major news
